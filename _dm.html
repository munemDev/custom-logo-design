          Who gets the most value from DesignManta?

          You have a real offer and customers, but your site or brand still makes you look smaller than you are. You want one accountable team, clear scope and a preview before big spend.

          Which platform do you build on?

          We use a modern visual site builder that's fast, secure and easy for you to edit later. No plugins to update.

          Do I pay anything up front?

          No. You pay the first month when you book, then monthly after that.

          Is the design really custom?

          Yes. Every site starts from a blank page based on your brief.

          How long does it take?

          Fourteen days from the moment your brief and content are in.

          When does the fourteen days start?

          On the kickoff date we agree together, once your brief is complete.

          Do you use AI to make the site?

          No. Our designers and writers make every design and copy decision.

          Do I need a logo first?

          It helps, but it isn't required. We can make a simple wordmark to get you started.

          What if I don't have photos or text ready?

          We write the copy and can source quality stock photos until you have your own.

          Can I cancel the monthly plan?

          The plan runs for 12 months. If something isn't working, talk to us and we'll find a fair solution.

          What does one feedback round mean?

          You collect all your changes into a single list and we apply them together.

          What if I hate the preview?

          You get a full refund. We’d rather lose a project than have you pay for work you don’t want. That’s the whole point of preview-first.

          What happens to hosting after a year?

          Renew with us, or move the site to your own account and pay the platform directly.

          What if I need more than one page?

          Extra pages can be added for a small one-off fee. Ask us for a quote.
