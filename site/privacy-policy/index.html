Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Logo Design is an independent branding and design studio. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or order our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Brief details you submit, such as your company name, slogan, industry, keywords, email address and (optionally) phone number.
  • Messages you send us by email or contact forms.
  • Payment details, which are processed by our payment provider. We never store full card numbers.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To prepare quotes, deliver design work and communicate about your project.
  • To process payments and keep accurate business records.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To send occasional updates, only where you have agreed to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers that help us run the business (for example hosting, email, analytics and payment processing), or when required by law.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the site work and analytics cookies to understand how it is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as expected if you do.

Data retention & security

We keep project information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting obligations. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, but no online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to certain uses. To make a request, email us at hello@logodesign.com.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@logodesign.com.
